## 🧠 What is GEO?

**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** is the practice of improving a website's visibility, citations, and recommendations in AI-powered search and answer engines such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, DeepSeek, and Google AI Overviews.

Unlike traditional SEO that targets keyword rankings on Google, GEO focuses on:
- **Entity clarity** — making sure AI systems understand who you are and what you do
- **Structured data** — providing machine-readable context via Schema.org markup
- **Machine-readable files** — llms.txt, llms-full.txt, robots.txt, sitemap.xml
- **Citation-worthiness** — being referenced as a trusted source by LLMs
- **Brand entity building** — establishing your brand as an authoritative entity
